# LinkedIn About: Proposed Rewrite

Proposal only. Nothing has been changed on LinkedIn.

## The reference style (from the profile you shared)

| Trait | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **No hook line** | Opens with "I am a Senior … with X years…", not a slogan. |
| **Plain, calm voice** | No emojis, no bold Unicode, no "here's what makes me different", no hype. |
| **Four short paragraphs, no bullets** | 1) who you are and where you work, 2) how you work, 3) strengths and collaboration, 4) open to plus contact. |
| **Ownership words** | "own", "deliver", "end to end". It shows responsibility rather than listing tasks. |
| **Few numbers** | Confidence comes from scope, not a list of metrics. |
| **Short close** | One line about what you're open to, and a plain-text email. |
| **Length** | About 1,000–1,500 characters. All of it fits on screen with little scrolling. |

## What changed compared with your current About

| Current | Proposed | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Hook: "Most software looks great in the demo…" | Opens with role, years, company and scope | Matches the reference style, and recruiters get the key facts in the first two lines (what shows before "…see more"). |
| "3+ years" / "4+ years" | 4+ years | Matches Experience. |
| 5-bullet metrics list with bold Unicode numbers | 2 numbers (99.9%, 70%) woven into sentences | Experience already holds the full metrics. The About now explains *how* you work. |
| "Here's what makes my work different: most engineers…" | "I design for the failure path first." | Same idea, without comparing yourself to other engineers. |
| Side projects as a bullet list (3 items) | One sentence with PostCraft and DevTools | Lighter. address-bd is still in Projects. |
| "Promoted twice, Junior to Senior" | Dropped | The Experience section shows it clearly. Add it back if you want it. |
| Email in bold Unicode | Plain-text email | People can copy it and search engines can find it. |
| "If that's your world, follow along." | Dropped | The reference style ends on what you're open to, not a follow call. |
