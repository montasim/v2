# Portfolio

This context describes the professional records presented by the portfolio and the ways visitors explore them.

## Language

**Portfolio Record**:
A validated entry describing one professional fact, such as a role, project, credential, education record, recommendation, skill group, or affiliation.
_Avoid_: Item, entity

**Catalog**:
An ordered collection of one kind of Portfolio Record, including the classifications and featured selections used to present it.
_Avoid_: List, dataset

**Catalog Filter**:
A visitor-selected classification that narrows a Catalog without changing its record order.
_Avoid_: Tab, category state

**Featured Record**:
A Portfolio Record explicitly selected for the overview rather than inferred from its position or identifier.
_Avoid_: Selected item, highlighted ID

**Credential**:
A professional certification with an issuer, proof URL, preview, and optional downloadable evidence.
_Avoid_: Certificate item

**Recommendation**:
Attributed professional feedback with a relationship, date, and explicit filter year.
_Avoid_: Testimonial
